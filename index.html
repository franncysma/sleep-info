Why Sleep Is Important

Sleep plays an essential role in overall well-being. During sleep, the body performs important processes related to recovery, focus, and balance.

Maintaining consistent sleep habits is often associated with improved daily performance and quality of life.

What Can Affect Sleep Quality

Several everyday factors may influence sleep quality, such as:

  • Stress and mental tension
  • Irregular sleep schedules
  • Excessive screen use before bedtime
  • Diet and late-night eating habits

Habits That Support Better Sleep

Many people focus on creating calming routines before bedtime. Reducing noise, lowering lights, and keeping a regular schedule are common practices that support better rest.
